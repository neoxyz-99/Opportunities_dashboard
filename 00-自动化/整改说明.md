# Verified Collection v2

The weekly schedule and gpt-4.1-mini model are unchanged. Long emails remain off.

## Collection

- `sources.json` is the executable source registry. Register an official URL, explicit domains and an opportunity kind. `render: true` is only for permitted public JavaScript pages; it is never used for LinkedIn.
- The collector reads robots.txt, checks public HTTPS addresses and fetches official pages. It queues up to twelve job detail links or six event links per source, prioritizes actionable calls and junior policy roles, and rotates attempted pages. Academic, youth, event, fellowship, scholarship, school and job sources receive equal turns. The overall model budget is unchanged. Indexed discovery covers all these categories, not only LinkedIn job leads. Standalone study/research scholarships have their own category; conference travel grants stay with their conference. Graduate-job screening is not applied to fellowship/scholarship experience requirements.
- Only new/changed document hashes are sent for structured extraction. Defaults: at most seven model requests (including one indexed discovery request), eighteen extraction pages and two search tool calls per weekly run. Deferred pages are shown as partial coverage, not silently dropped.
- LinkedIn is a public search-index discovery channel only. Direct LinkedIn access, login scraping and mailbox ingestion are disabled. Search leads must be verified on a registered official employer domain.
- `collection_status.json` separates actual collection health from page generation. Source failures and extraction failures are retained. A failed collector exits nonzero; Actions still publishes the failure report and then fails the job.
- The public dashboard only shows a short collection status, not the source error list. Full reports remain in Actions and history files. A missing page decision rejects that page only; supported records from other pages survive. Per-batch rejection diagnostics are retained separately.
- `collection_cache.json` stores validated document hashes, not credentials. Do not delete it routinely: doing so forces re-extraction and increases cost.
- `--probe` reads real sources without model calls or database updates. Its separate report is not treated as a successful collection.

## Eligibility

`资格条件JSON` contains category, Required/Preferred/Not stated, English summary, exact source quote, evidence URL and explicitly stated minimum work experience. An unsupported quote or invented application URL rejects that record, without discarding valid records from the same batch. US location does not imply citizenship; sponsorship and OPT/CPT are separate facts. China school and party-membership requirements are published only when stated by the organiser. No personal political affiliation or immigration status is inferred.

Early-career Jobs targets graduates and zero-experience applicants. Explicitly required prior professional experience, senior roles and unrelated coordination/administration are hidden from the default view. Preferred experience and unspecified experience do not automatically exclude an opportunity.

## Compatibility

Old database rows are retained. Display normalization does not update verification dates. Existing matched records keep discovery date, fingerprint and status, preserving per-browser archive selections. A new explicit annual cohort is a separate record. Unsupported legacy requirements are labelled as not reverified, not converted into eligibility evidence.

## Validation

Run `python -m unittest discover -s tests -v` and the existing sample validator. After installing Chromium, `python tests/browser_check.py` checks responsive layout, filters and archive/restore. Use `python 00-自动化/collect_opportunities.py --probe` for live-source connectivity; a full run also needs OPENAI_API_KEY. A green Page build is not evidence that collection succeeded: inspect collection status, new_count and the source report.

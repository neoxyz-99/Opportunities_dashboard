# Collection Coverage Audit: 30 September 2026

The three recent automatic additions were academic calls, not jobs. They were collected on 30 September after an extended period of ineffective collection; the thirty-day counter is not evidence that the new pipeline ran successfully for a full month.

## Confirmed causes

- The last full run checked 35 registered sources: 17 failed and 18 had partial coverage. Seven model requests and eighteen extraction pages are a bounded weekly sample, not comprehensive coverage.
- The old source selection took only three links per source. WRI currently exposes 21 actual job detail links; policy internships were competing with senior roles and generic links.
- CGD used an old website path and the wrong recruitment-system domain. Its currently linked ApplyToJob site is readable and exposes a Research Assistant vacancy.
- A missing model decision for one page invalidated the whole batch. It now invalidates only the missing or duplicate page. Exact source evidence remains mandatory.
- The backlog counted links, not qualified jobs. For example, Earth System Governance contributed 259 links, including publication and archive pages; these paths are now excluded from the opportunity queue.
- Failed pages had no attempt timestamp and could monopolize the same source slots repeatedly. Attempt timestamps now support rotation without falsely caching a failed extraction as valid.
- Indexed search covered recruitment only. It now includes academic CFPs, youth participation, forums, fellowships, scholarships and policy schools. Youth participation had no dedicated source group; the UN Youth Office and MGCY are now executable youth sources, not UN internship sources. All seven source kinds receive equal queue turns. EUI Max Weber and DAAD EPOS are added as separate fellowship/scholarship sources; standalone scholarships have their own dashboard category. Conference travel grants stay in the conference category. Fellowship/scholarship experience requirements are shown, not suppressed by the zero-experience job filter.
- Explicit 'no work experience required' could fail numeric-year validation. Explicit zero-experience evidence is now accepted without requiring the word 'year'.

## Live evidence, no model calls

- WRI: listing and individual job pages readable; 21 specific current job links.
- CGD: official-linked recruitment site and Research Assistant detail readable.
- E3G: one currently visible position, a senior internal opportunity requiring three to five years; appropriately outside the graduate scope.
- GCF: official programme pages readable, but its specific vacancy portal remained unreadable in both ordinary and permitted rendered tests. This remains an unresolved source adapter/access limitation.
- IPPA: its official home page and currently open ICPP8 panel call were read successfully. Book/publication/archive links are excluded; a current call is distinct from a conference news page. The panel call has been manually validated and imported, with its higher preparation burden and separate later paper phase clearly identified.
- UN Youth Office: the official home page and Townhall detail were read successfully. The December 2026 Townhall funding deadline was 7 September 2026 and was missed by the previous pipeline; it is not imported as an open application now.
- MGCY: nine youth-participation candidate links and one detail were read successfully. These are leads, not nine currently open applications; the checked listing contains earlier 2026 events.
- DAAD EPOS: scholarship detail and a linked detail were read successfully; seven candidate links, not seven awards. Minimum professional experience and eligible-country restrictions must be extracted, not assumed absent. EUI Max Weber was verified in the public index but the direct robots check timed out, so automated readability is not confirmed. Humboldt Climate Protection is also registered as a future-cycle watch source; its previous deadline has passed and the next deadline must not be inferred from an annual pattern.

One manually reviewed WRI Restoration Finance & Policy internship was validated through the same source-evidence checks and deduplication. It requires existing US work authorization, offers no employer visa sponsorship and requires DC/MD/VA residence at the start of employment. OPT/CPT acceptance is not explicitly stated. The page header says 3 October and the application instructions say 2 October; the earlier date is used and the inconsistency is visible in the judgment. This is thematic fit, not confirmation of the user's eligibility.

## Remaining limits

The weekly model-call and page budgets have not been increased. Discovery and prioritization across all opportunity types are improved, but not every source or opportunity can be processed within those limits. Restricted websites need an officially allowed feed or recruitment-system adapter; browser security, robots restrictions and login requirements are not bypassed. Public search indexing does not guarantee LinkedIn completeness. A successful dashboard-only deployment verifies publication and tests, not a new full model-backed collection.

## How to expand systematically

1. Start with disciplinary associations, then follow their working groups, regional branches and postgraduate networks. The BISA news page directly links these scattered calls.
2. Track conference submission, attendance registration and travel support separately. A conference date alone does not establish that participation is still open.
3. Use funding councils and institute programme directories to discover recurring fellowships; then verify each current cohort on its official page.
4. Add university institutes as the next layer: [NUS ARI](https://ari.nus.edu.sg/research-node-global-indo-pacific/) is a relevant Asian studies / international politics lead, not yet an executable registry source in this release.
5. Use [H-Net announcements](https://networks.h-net.org/h-announce) as discovery leads, not an unlimited trusted-domain gateway. Its automatic robots check is currently failing; prefer an approved feed or manual link submission until resolved.
6. Newsletter and indexed LinkedIn leads need the official call / application page before admission. This release does not read private email, subscribe accounts or scrape logged-in LinkedIn.

## Implemented in this release

- Executable source registry: 40 to 48 publishers/programme entries, including eight additional non-recruitment sources.
- Explicit official sub-entry URLs are now queued for extraction, not merely mentioned in the prompt.
- Categories remain balanced; publishers within a category rotate by their latest real extraction attempt, including failed attempts.
- The paid budget remains seven model calls / 18 changed detail pages per run. This budget cannot guarantee full coverage of 48 sources every week.
- Source/application/evidence URLs are constrained to supplied URLs in the output schema, preventing prose application links and citations to unread documents.
- Mandatory CCP membership is hidden across all opportunity types. Preferred / unstated membership and academic society membership remain eligible for display. Rows and archive IDs are retained.

## Readability and limits

The new entries were checked from the local network without paid model calls. This is not proof that all work from GitHub's runner. The original UNFCCC fellowship URL was unreadable; the officially published UNU partner page is readable and explicitly says the fourth cohort is closed.

World Bank Youth Summit 2026 and IIAS Asia in the World 2026 calls are also past-cycle sources, retained to detect subsequent announcements rather than advertised as open opportunities.

403/CAPTCHA/robots restrictions are not bypassed. Use permitted official alternative pages, feeds or APIs; where the cloud runner alone is blocked, an explicitly configured local collector is a separate deployment decision, not implemented automatically here.

The dashboard shows compact health only. Full technical source diagnostics remain in the repository. A green dashboard-only run proves generation/deployment, not successful new collection.
